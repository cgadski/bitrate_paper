We now return to our toy scenario. Let $F$ be a dictionary of $N$ codewords drawn independently and uniformly from the unit sphere in $\R^d.$ Let $b(d, k, N)$ denote the maximum probability that a superposition code for a $k$-element is \textit{not} accurately recovered by one-step thresholding at level $\tau$ for any $\tau.$

Our first result provides an asymptotic constraint on the dimension $d$ required for $b(d, k, N)$ to be small.

\begin{proposition} \label{prop:necessary-cond}
    Let $\epsilon > 0$ be arbitrary. Over the regime
    $$
    d \le (1 - \epsilon) 2 k \ln N,
    $$
    it holds that $\liminf_{N \to \infty} b(d, k, N) > 0.$
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition} \label{prop:sufficient-cond}
	For $\eta \in (0, 1),$ let $\ln k \le \eta \ln N.$ Then for any $\epsilon > 0,$ over the regime
	$$
	d \ge (1 + \epsilon) (2 + 4 \sqrt \eta + 2 \eta) k \ln N,
	$$
	it holds that $\lim_{N \to \infty} b(d, k, N) = 0.$
\end{proposition}
