We now return to our toy scenario. Let $F$ be a dictionary of $N$ codewords drawn independently and uniformly from the unit sphere in $\R^d.$

\begin{definition}
Let $b(\tau, d, k, N)$ denote the probability that a superposition code for a $k$-element is not accurately recovered by threshold decoding at level $\tau,$ and let
$$
b(d, k, N) = \inf_\tau b(\tau, d, k, N).
$$
\end{definition}

Our first result provides an asymptotic constraint on the dimension $d$ required for $b(d, k, N)$ to be small.

\begin{proposition} \label{prop:necessary-cond}
    Let $\epsilon > 0$ be arbitrary. Over the regime
    $$
    d \le (1 - \epsilon) 2 k \ln N,
    $$
    it holds that $\liminf_{N \to \infty} \inf_\tau b(\tau, d, k, N) > 0.$
\end{proposition}

When $k \ge \epsilon N$ for some $\epsilon > 0,$ note that
$$
\ln \binom{N}{k} \ge ... = O(N)
$$
while \Cref{prop:necessary-cond} shows that threshold decoding requires $d = \Omega(k \ln N) = \Omega(N \ln N).$ We conclude that the minimum ``dimensions per bit'' $\frac d H$ tends to infinity for large $N.$

However, it turns out that threshold decoding

\begin{proposition} \label{prop:sufficient-cond}
	For $\eta \in (0, 1),$ let $\ln k \le \eta \ln N.$ Then for any $\epsilon > 0,$ over the regime
	$$
	d \ge (1 + \epsilon) (2 + 4 \sqrt \eta + 2 \eta) k \ln N,
	$$
	it holds that $\lim_{N \to \infty} b(d, k, N) = 0.$
\end{proposition}

This result has a remarkable interpretation in terms of effective ``channel capacity.''
