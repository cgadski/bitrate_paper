\section{Proof of \Cref{prop:necessary-cond} \label{appendix:necessary}}

In our discussion of Gaussian channels in \Cref{sec:information}, we recalled that when an individual coordinate $X$ of unit variance suffers interference of power $P,$ it can convey at most $\ln(1 + P^{-1})/2$ nats to a receiver. In terms of mutual information, this means that
$$
I(X; X + Z) \le \frac 1 2 \ln (1 + P^{-1}) = \frac{1}{2 P} + O(P^{-2})
$$
for large $P.$ We begin by establishing a similar bound on mutual information between a superposition of Rademacher codewords and one of its summands.

\begin{proposition} \label{prop:mutual}
Let $F_1, \dots, F_k$ be $k$ independent Rademacher codewords, as in the definition of a Rademacher dictionary. Then for any random variable $Y$ supported on the set $\{-1/\sqrt{d}, 1/\sqrt{d}\}^d,$
$$
I\left(Y; Y + \sum_{i = 1}^k F_k \right) \le d \left(\frac 1 {2 k} + O(k^{-2})\right),
$$
where the remainder of $O(k^{-2})$ depends only on $k.$
\end{proposition}

\color{blue} \begin{proof}
Let $d, k, N \ge 1$ be any integers. Let $F \in \R^{d \times (N + k)}$ be a Rademacher dictionary, and let $(F^0, F^1)$ denote the sub-matrices of $F$ containing the first $N$ and the last $k$ columns respectively. Let $K \in [N]$ be uniformly random, independent from $F,$ and let $X \in \R^{N + k}$ have coordinates
$$
X_i = \begin{cases}
1 : i = K \lor i > N \\
0 : \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
Denote $(X^0, X^1)$ for its first $N$ and last $k$ coordinates of $X,$ so that $F X = F^0 X^0 + F^1 X^1.$ Finally, define a function $G \colon \R^{d} \to [N]$ by
$$
G(y) = \arg \max_{i \in [N]} \langle y, F_i \rangle,
$$
breaking ties arbitrarily.

If top-$k$ decoding succeeds at decoding $Y$ from $F X,$ then we must have $\langle y, F_K \rangle \ge \langle y, F_i \rangle$ for any $i \in [N].$ It follows by rearrangement
$$
b(d, k + 1, N + k; \text{top-}k) \ge \Pp(G(F(X)) \neq K).
$$
However, by a Fano inequality,
$$
\Pp(G(F(Y)) \neq K) \ge 1 - \frac{\ln 2 + I(F^0 X^0; F X)}{\ln N}.
$$
Finally, note that $F^1 X^1$ is a sum of $k$ Rademacher vectors that is independent from the vector $F^0 X^0 \in \{-1/\sqrt{d}, 1/\sqrt{d} \}.$ Therefore, apply \Cref{prop:mutual} to bound $I(F^0 X^0; F X)$ lets us conclude that
$$
b(d, k + 1, N + k; \tau) \ge 1 - \frac{\ln 2}{\ln N} - \frac{d}{\ln N}\left( \frac{1} {2 (k + 1)} + O(k^{-2})\right),
$$

\end{proof}
