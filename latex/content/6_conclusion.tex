\section{Conclusions and Future Work}

Over the course of computation, it is common for programs to use more memory than is required to store their input data. Similarly, it is natural to expect that the entropy of an ``interpretable'' description for the residual vectors within a language model would be greater than the entropy of language.

Attempts to provide such interpretable descriptions using sparse autoencoders are limited for reasons that are not yet well understood. One basic question is whether the simple methods currently used to perform sparse reconstruction are capable of decoding all the information that may be present in a superposition code.

Overall, we hope the point of view of coding efficiency helps guide the interpretation of neural representations in future work.
