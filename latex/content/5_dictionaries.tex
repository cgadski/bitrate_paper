\section{Are Random Dictionaries Optimal? \label{sec:dictionaries}}

In the last section, we considered the performance of threshold and top-$k$ decodings at recovering a subset from a superposition code with a \textit{random} dictionary $F$. One natural question is whether these one-step decodings can do better if the dictionary is optimized in some way.

First, recall the notion of mutual coherence
$$
\mu(F) = \max_{i \neq j} \lvert \langle f_i, f_j \rangle \rvert
$$
of a dictionary $F = \{f_1, \dots, f_N\}.$
