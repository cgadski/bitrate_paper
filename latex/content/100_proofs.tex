\section{Proofs of Main Results \label{appendix:proofs}}

We begin with \Cref{prop:necessary-cond}, restated here for convenience.

\begin{proposition*}
    Let $\epsilon > 0$ be arbitrary. Over the regime
    $$
    d \le (1 - \epsilon) 2 k \ln N,
    $$
    it holds that $\liminf_{N \to \infty} \inf_\tau b(\tau, d, k, N) > 0.$
\end{proposition*}
